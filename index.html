About

Dubai-based photographer and filmmaker with a focus on cinematic visual storytelling.

Since 2023 I've built a practice across commercial and personal work — shooting product campaigns, restaurant promos, and video ads. Alongside freelance work I served as head of media for GEMS Founders School, leading a team of 27 across all media operations for the school and GFSMUN conferences in the academic year 2025-2026.

Over my three years of media experience and 17 official media roles in my school, I've come to find that this is all I really want to do in life, because cinematography and picture allow me to convey the emotions that I wouldn't otherwise have the courage to show, and it's enabled me to create extraordinary moments with my friends and family. The goal is to be a Director of Photography for major motion productions one day, and I hope to see you at the movies.
